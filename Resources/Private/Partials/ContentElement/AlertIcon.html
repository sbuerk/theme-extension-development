The glyph of one ".theme-alert" kind, for the "theme-alert__icon" slot. The alert contract (Resources/Private/Scss/components/_alert.scss) makes the glyph, not the colour, what tells the kinds apart - forced colours drop the tint and the accent and leave only this. One per kind, copied from the specimens in "Partials/Styleguide/Boxes.html" so a notice on a content page and the styleguide draw the same six. An unknown kind gets the note's glyph, the same fallback "ThemeNotice.html" applies to the kind itself.
