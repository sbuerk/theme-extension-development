The card and thumbnail layouts of the two page menus, "menu_pages" and "menu_subpages": one ".theme-card" per page, in a ".theme-card-grid" (Resources/Private/Scss/components/_card.scss). Arguments: "data" (the content element record), "items" (the menu of "MenuProcessor"), "label" (the name of the navigation) and "thumbnails" (1 for the thumbnails, anything else for the cards). The items come from the second "MenuProcessor" of the element, the one with a nested "FilesProcessor" on the page media - "item.files.0" is the first file of the page's "media" field, with the alternative text of its reference. A page without media gets a card without "__media". The rest of an item is the shape every menu template reads: "item.title" (the navigation title where there is one), "item.link", "item.current" and the page row at "item.data", "abstract" included. The title is the link of the card, and "--linked" stretches it over the card: the page title is the only name the link can have, and a separate "read more" link would repeat it. It sits one level below the heading of the element ("ItemHeading.html"), and carries "aria-current" for the current page, as every menu does. A card shows the abstract, a thumbnail only the image and the title, as a "--compact" card in a "--narrow" grid.
