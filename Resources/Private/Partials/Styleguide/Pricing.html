Styleguide section "Pricing": ".theme-pricing", the component "theme_pricing" renders its plans on. The rules of every other section apply: literal markup copied from the markup contract in "components/_pricing.scss", nothing driven by a record, a data processor or an "f:cObject", and literal English copy. Three plans, because three is where the grid has something to say: they share one row above 48rem and stack below it, and the middle one is the highlighted plan, so the modifier is shown next to the plans it is meant to be compared against rather than on its own. The plan names are "h4": the page owns h1, the section h2, and "h3" is already the specimen heading below - the same outline reason "Boxes.html" gives. In a rendered content element they follow the heading level of the element instead ("Partials/ContentElement/ItemHeading.html"). The prices are deliberately of different widths - 0, 19 and 49 - because the point of "font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums" is that the digits line up across the three columns. The feature lists are deliberately of different lengths, which is what pushes the buttons apart unless "__actions" keeps them on one line. The third plan has no button: a plan an editor left without a link ends with its features, and the two beside it keep their own buttons at the bottom of their own box rather than lining up with nothing.

Pricing

Plans side by side, to be read across rather than down. Above 48rem the plans share one row in equal columns, so a reader compares the same line of three plans at a glance; below it they stack. The prices are set in tabular figures, which is what makes the digits line up between the columns, and every button sits on the same line whatever the feature lists above them did.

Three plans, the middle one highlighted
