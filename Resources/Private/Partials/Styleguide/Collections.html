Styleguide section "collections" - components that arrange several items of one kind: the card grid in its column counts and as a scroller, cards whose title is the link, the timeline and the list group. Contracts: "Resources/Private/Scss/components/_card.scss", "components/_timeline.scss" and "components/_list-group.scss". The bare card and the auto-fit grid stay in Boxes, where they were; this section is what the collections add on top of them. Literal markup only, specimen copy in English, headings one level below the section heading like every other section - see the comment at the top of "Boxes.html" for the heading levels and for why the media are data URIs, which the two variables below repeat: a partial is rendered on its own by the visual suite and carries its own. The scroller is a region with a tab stop and a name, as the contract requires - "scrollable-region-focusable" is one of the rules axe runs on this page. The icon of the timeline is an icon of the set, rendered by ""; the ring of every other entry is geometry of the stylesheet - see "docs/development/icons.md#the-rule".

Card grid, three columns

--columns-3 caps the grid at three columns and keeps the minimum width of a column, so the row still breaks into fewer on a narrow screen. The cards are items of a list, a subtitle follows the title, and each card ends in a button of its own style, pushed to the foot of the card.

Split tiles

A column of featurettes: a picture on one side and the words on the other, with the side changing from tile to tile. Nothing in the markup of a tile says which side it is on — the stylesheet alternates with :nth-child(even), so inserting or reordering a tile keeps the rhythm right, and --reversed on the list starts it on the other foot. Each tile carries its own tone, the same three the call to action offers. The tiles stack below the breakpoint, where there is no room for two halves.

Card scroller

One row that scrolls sideways and snaps each card to the start. A card is a third of the strip less a quarter, so the next one shows at the end. The strip is a region with a name and a tab stop: focus it and the arrow keys scroll it, with no script involved.

Timeline

An ordered list on an axis at the start of the line: a ring on the first line of every entry and a hairline down to the next, and an icon of the set in place of the ring where an entry has one. The date is a time element with a machine readable value.

List group

A framed list of rows. The title is the one link of a row, and the whole row is its target; hovering it fills it, and the focus ring frames the row rather than the title. The meta data sits at the end of the row, and moves to a line of its own where the row is too narrow. The last row has no link and is no target.
