The newsletter band of the "newsletter" footer variant: a heading, an optional line of text, and a button leading to the page that carries the actual subscription form. A LINK, NOT A FORM. A newsletter form needs somewhere to post to, and the theme has nowhere - it ships no controller, no storage and no double opt-in, and every one of those is a requirement rather than a detail for an address somebody types in. A form field that looks like a subscription and drops what is typed into it is worse than no field at all, so the band leads to the page where the real form lives, whichever extension renders it there. Three settings, and the band needs all three: "theme.footer.newsletterHeading", "theme.footer.newsletterPage" and "theme.footer.newsletterLabel". A fourth, "newsletterText", is the one optional part - a band with a heading and a button says enough on its own. Missing any of the three renders nothing: a band with no heading has nothing to say, and a band whose button has no destination or no name is a control that misleads. See "Partials/Page/Header/Action.html" for the same rule and for why the destination is a page uid rather than a link. The heading level is "h2": the band sits in the footer, outside the content of the page, and is a section of the document rather than a part of the article - the site footer carries no other heading to nest under.
