Header variant "actions": the site title, a call to action and the controls in the first row, the main navigation on a second row below. The call to action is the point of the variant, and it is why the navigation moves down: a third thing beside the menu in the one-row header would push the width at which the menu collapses further out - see "Partials/Page/Header.html". Here it is the navigation that leaves, so the row the call to action joins holds the title and the controls and nothing else, and the menu has a row of its own. "Page/Header/Action.html" renders nothing at all unless both of its settings are configured, so this variant without a configured call to action is simply a two-row header. That is a usable state rather than a broken one, and it is what a site gets that switches the variant on before filling in the two values.
