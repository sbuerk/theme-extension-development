Header variant "centred": the site title centred on a row of its own, with the controls at the end of that row, and the main navigation centred on a second row below it. Two rows, so two "__inner" blocks rather than one with a wrapping flex layout: each row is centred and spaced on its own terms, and a reader of the markup can see which things share a row. "layout/_site-header.scss" styles them through the "--centred" modifier on the header. The title keeps a whole row here, so the 50% cap the one-row header needs between the two breakpoints does not apply - see "Partials/Page/Header.html". The controls sit in the title's row and not in the navigation's, because they belong to the site rather than to the menu, and because the navigation row is the one that grows with the number of entries.
