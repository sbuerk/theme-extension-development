"theme_features" - one feature per row of "tx_theme_list_items", on ".theme-feature" in a ".theme-feature-grid" (Resources/Private/Scss/components/_feature.scss). The "features" examples of Bootstrap, the "icon group" of bootstrap_package. --- "layout" is the item layout ----------------------------------------- The core column "layout", re-enabled and relabelled for this CType in "Configuration/PageTsConfig/ContentElementAppearance.tsconfig": 0 Columns ".theme-feature--column", the icon on a tile above 1 Hanging icons ".theme-feature--hanging", a framed tile beside 2 Icon tiles ".theme-feature--tile", every feature a framed box 3 With an intro ".theme-feature--hanging" in a ".theme-feature-intro", the heading and the text of the element beside the grid "tx_theme_columns" is the most columns the grid takes, "--columns-2" to "--columns-4". Any other value of either - 0 included, which is what v13.4 gives the integer column of a record written outside the form - renders the first case: layout 0 and three columns, the TCA defaults.
