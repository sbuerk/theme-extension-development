"theme_steps" - one step per row of "tx_theme_list_items", on ".theme-steps" (Resources/Private/Scss/components/_steps.scss): an ordered list, because the order of the items is the content. The marker of a step is empty and numbered by the stylesheet, so no number in the markup can disagree with the order of the list. A step whose child carries a usable "icon" shows the icon in its marker instead, with the modifier "theme-steps__marker--icon"; the icon is rendered with "optional" into a variable first - see "ThemeTextIcon.html". The marker is "aria-hidden": the list says which item of how many a step is. The title is one level below the element's heading ("Partials/ContentElement/ItemHeading.html"), the text the child's plain "text" through "f:format.nl2br". "item.data.*": "DatabaseQueryProcessor" wraps every row, see "ThemeMediaTeaserGrid.html".
