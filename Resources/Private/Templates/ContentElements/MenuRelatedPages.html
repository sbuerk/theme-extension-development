The "Related pages" menu - "special = keywords" in "ContentElements.typoscript" ("tt_content.menu_related_pages") matches the page "pages" resolves to (or, empty, the current page) against every other page sharing at least one of its own "keywords". Same shared partial as "menu_pages", with neither "showAbstract" nor "showDate" - a related page is a plain link, the same as a page-selected or subpages menu.
