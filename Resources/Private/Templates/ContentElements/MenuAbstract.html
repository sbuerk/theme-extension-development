The "Abstracts" menu - "menu_subpages" (the children of the selected "pages") with each page's own "abstract" field shown beneath its link. "abstract" needs no "dataProcessing" of its own to reach: "MenuProcessor" already puts the full page row - a "'*'"-selected "pages" record, "abstract" included - at "item.data" for every item ("MenuProcessor::getDataAsJson()", ".Build/vendor/typo3/cms-frontend/Classes/DataProcessing/ MenuProcessor.php:495"), so passing "showAbstract: 1" into the shared partial is the only thing this template does differently from "menu_subpages".
