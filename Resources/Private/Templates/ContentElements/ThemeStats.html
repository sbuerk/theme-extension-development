"theme_stats" - one figure per row of "tx_theme_list_items", on ".theme-stat" in a ".theme-stats" description list (Resources/Private/Scss/components/_stat.scss). The child's "subheader" is what the figure counts, the term of the list; its "header" is the figure, the first description; its plain "text" a second description, only when there is one. Both "header" and "subheader" are required on this relation ("tt_content_theme_stats.php"), and a row without one of them - a record still mid-edit, or written outside the form - renders nothing rather than an empty "dt" or "dd", as the definition list of the bullet list skips a row without its term. The comparison is with the empty string, not a truth test, so a figure "0" is a figure. The stylesheet shows the figure above the term; the source keeps the order a description list reads in. The icon is the child's "icon", decoration before the figure, rendered with "optional" into a variable first - see "ThemeTextIcon.html". "item.data.*": "DatabaseQueryProcessor" wraps every row, see "ThemeMediaTeaserGrid.html".
