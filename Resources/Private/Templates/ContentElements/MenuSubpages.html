The "Subpages" menu - one link per direct child of the pages the editor selected in "pages" (not the selected pages themselves). "special = directory" in "ContentElements.typoscript" ("tt_content.menu_subpages") is what tells "MenuProcessor" to fetch the children rather than the pages themselves; an empty "pages" selection falls back to the current page's own children. Same shared partial as "menu_pages" - the difference between the two is entirely the TypoScript "special". "layout" picks the rendering: 1 cards, 2 thumbnails - both "Partials/ContentElement/MenuCards.html", fed by the second "MenuProcessor" of the element, which adds the page media (see "lib.themeMenuWithPageMedia" in "ContentElements.typoscript") - and anything else the list. The page TSconfig offers the field on this CType and on "menu_pages", where the same three values apply.
