The "Subpages" menu - one link per direct child of the pages the editor selected in "pages" (not the selected pages themselves). "special = directory" in "ContentElements.typoscript" ("tt_content.menu_subpages") is what tells "MenuProcessor" to fetch the children rather than the pages themselves; an empty "pages" selection falls back to the current page's own children. Same shared partial as "menu_pages" - the difference between the two is entirely the TypoScript "special".
