"theme_sociallinks" - the same list of links as "theme_linklist" ("ThemeLinklist.html"), rendered through the identical ".theme-content-menu"/"ContentElement/LinkList" pair rather than a component of its own - the two CTypes only differ in the child table's "link" restriction ("allowedTypes" in "Configuration/TCA/Overrides/tt_content_theme_sociallinks.php") and the "aria-label" below, not in shape. Every entry renders as a text label, "link_label" standing in for what would otherwise be a platform icon ("Mastodon", "LinkedIn", ...). The theme's icons are the solid style of Font Awesome Free, and platform logos are in its brands style, which is deliberately not vendored - see "docs/development/icons.md". Recorded here as the gap it is: an icon-driven variant is not something this template approximates with a Unicode glyph or a look-alike solid icon, it is simply not rendered.
