"theme_sociallinks" - a row of links to the site's profiles on other platforms, each named by a logo of the vendored Font Awesome Free brands set ("docs/development/icons.md"). Markup contract: docs/development/component-library.md#social-links. It shares the child table with "theme_linklist" ("ThemeLinklist.html") and differs from it in three things, which is why it has a component of its own rather than the ".theme-content-menu" it used before the logos were vendored: the child's "link" is restricted to the types a profile link can have ("allowedTypes" in "Configuration/TCA/Overrides/tt_content_theme_sociallinks.php"), the row is horizontal, and an entry with a logo collapses to a square. The "
" and its "aria-label" are this template's, the list is "ContentElement/SocialLinks.html" - the same split the link list uses.
