"theme_notice" - a notice on ".theme-alert", in one of the six kinds of "tx_theme_notice_kind" (Configuration/TCA/Overrides/tt_content.php). The kind decides the modifier and the "role", and the two are decided together here so they cannot disagree. The role follows the alert contract in docs/development/component-library.md: "info" and "success" are polite live regions ("status"), "warning" and "danger" assertive ones ("alert"), and "note" and "tip" are asides to the text around them and take "note" - never a live region. The modifier is written for "info" as well, although it is the look of the bare class: the contract lists it so a template can name it rather than rely on the default. A value outside the six - an import, a value from before a kind was removed - renders as a note, the kind the TCA defaults to, rather than as a modifier the stylesheet does not know next to a role nobody chose. "header" is the notice's title and renders as ".theme-alert__title" inside the component rather than through the shared header partial: it names the notice, it does not open a section of the page. "bodytext" is rich text, so ".theme-alert__text" is a "div" rather than the "p" the contract shows - "f:format.html" wraps its lines in paragraphs of its own, and a paragraph inside a paragraph is invalid. The class is selected by class, not by tag. A notice with neither a title nor a text renders nothing inside its wrapper: an empty box with "role=status" or "role=alert" is a live region announcing nothing, and the assertive kinds would interrupt a reader for it.
