"theme_teaser_list" - rows of teasers, on ".theme-list-group" (Resources/Private/Scss/components/_list-group.scss). The rows are "tx_theme_list_items", resolved like those of "ThemeMediaTeaserGrid.html", in the order of the relation. A row has one link, and the title is it: "ItemHeading" renders the title as "f:link.typolink" inside the heading, and the stylesheet stretches the link over the row, so the whole row is its target and nothing interactive is nested in anything else. A row without a link has a title of plain text and is no target. "link" is TCA type=link, so "f:link.typolink", never a plain "href" - see "Partials/ContentElement/LinkList.html". The image is an avatar, ".theme-avatar" (components/_avatar.scss), in its decorative form: it sits beside the visible title of the row, which names it, so it takes an empty "alt" rather than the alternative text of its file reference, which would be read out next to the title. It is written as an "img" of its own with "f:uri.image", so its attributes are exactly those of the avatar contract; the file is processed to 80 pixels square, twice the 40 of the avatar. The date is a "time" element, formatted like the date of "ThemeTimeline.html"; the meta data is plain text beside it. Neither is rendered when empty, and the whole "__meta" block is left out when both are.
