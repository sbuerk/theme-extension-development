"theme_tabs" - one tab per row of "tx_theme_list_items", on ".theme-tabs". The markup keeps the three rules of the tabs contract (Resources/Private/Scss/components/_tabs.scss) that the stylesheet and "Resources/Public/JavaScript/theme.js" both depend on: 1. The first tab is selected, every other one carries tabindex=-1. 2. A panel carries its class and its id and nothing else - no "hidden", no "role", no "aria-labelledby", no "tabindex". The script adds them when it binds the group; without the script every panel is shown. 3. Every panel repeats its tab's label as ".theme-tabs__heading", which labels the panel while there are no tabs. Ids are derived from the element's own anchor, "c{data.uid}", and the position of the row: unique per element because the uid is, and unique within it because the position is. Not from the uid of the child row - the anchor is the one identifier of an element the rest of the theme already treats as its name on the page (and "LegacyDeliveryTest" as the one thing a mirrored element may differ in). The tab list is named after the element's header, which is also rendered as its heading above the group; without a header it falls back to a generic label, because a tab list needs a name of its own. The panel heading is one level below the element's heading, so the outline continues rather than restarting: h3 under the default h2, and h2 when the element renders no heading at all. The level only matters without the script - bound, the stylesheet hides the heading and the tab labels the panel. "item.data.text" is rich text on this relation (see "tt_content_theme_tabs.php"), hence "f:format.html". "item.data.*" and not "item.*": "DatabaseQueryProcessor" wraps every row, see "ThemeMediaTeaserGrid.html".
