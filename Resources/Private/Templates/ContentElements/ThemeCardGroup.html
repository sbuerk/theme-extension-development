"theme_card_group" - cards in a grid or in one row that scrolls sideways, on ".theme-card-grid" (Resources/Private/Scss/components/_card.scss). The cards are "tx_theme_list_items", resolved like those of "ThemeMediaTeaserGrid.html" - see its comment for the "item.data" and "item.files" shape "DatabaseQueryProcessor" hands over. They are items of a list, a "ul" of "li.theme-card", and their titles sit one level below the heading of the element ("Partials/ContentElement/ItemHeading.html"). "tx_theme_columns" picks the column modifier, "layout" the arrangement: 1 is the scroller, 2 the wall, anything else the grid - the core value 3 is removed from the form for this type, and a record still carrying it renders the grid. A value of "tx_theme_columns" outside the three renders three columns, the default of the column. "ContentElementContractTest" holds every modifier written here to a rule of the compiled stylesheet. The wall is the same list of cards laid out in columns the cards stack into, so it needs no wrapper and no region: it does not scroll. It fills **column by column** rather than row by row, which the component's header comment states, justifies and bounds - it is why no element whose items are a sequence offers one. The scroller is the region of the contract, with a tab stop and a name: the heading of the element, and a generic label where there is none, so the region is never nameless. The heading is used although it may be hidden ("header_layout" 100) - it still says what the cards are. A card with a link renders it as a button of the style the editor picked, through "Partials/ContentElement/LinkButton.html", the partial the heroes and teasers render their link through. "item.data.text" is the plain text column of the child table, hence "f:format.nl2br". {data.header}
