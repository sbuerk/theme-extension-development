"theme_split_tiles" - alternating featurettes, on ".theme-split-tiles" (Resources/Private/Scss/components/_split-tiles.scss). The tiles are "tx_theme_list_items", resolved like those of "ThemeMediaTeaserGrid.html", in the order of the relation. They are the items of a list, a "ul" of "li", and their titles sit one level below the heading of the element ("Partials/ContentElement/ItemHeading.html"). **The markup of every tile is identical.** Which side the picture of a given tile is on is decided by the stylesheet, with ":nth-child(even)", and by nothing here - so inserting, deleting or reordering a tile in the backend keeps the rhythm right by itself, which a per-tile modifier written by this template would not. "layout" picks the foot the alternation starts on: 1 writes "--reversed" on the list, anything else the plain class. The two core values the element does not render are removed from the form for this type ("ContentElementAppearance.tsconfig"), and a record still carrying one starts on the default foot. "item.data.tone" is the tone of the individual tile - the three of ".theme-cta", matched value by value, anything else the surface fill and no modifier. "ContentElementContractTest" holds every modifier written here to a rule of the compiled stylesheet. The media is rendered before the body on every tile, whichever side it is painted on, so the reading order and the focus order are the same on all of them - see the component's header comment, and "_teaser.scss" for the same argument at length. A tile with a link renders it as a button of the style the editor picked, through "Partials/ContentElement/LinkButton.html". "item.data.text" is the plain text column of the child table, hence "f:format.nl2br".
