"theme_timeline" - dated entries on a line, on ".theme-timeline" (Resources/Private/Scss/components/_timeline.scss). The entries are "tx_theme_list_items", resolved like those of "ThemeMediaTeaserGrid.html" and sorted by their date in the TypoScript ("tt_content.theme_timeline"), so this template renders them in the order it gets them. An "ol": the order is part of what a timeline says. The date is a "time" element: "datetime" carries the ISO date, the visible text the ICU pattern "d MMMM y" in the locale of the site language - "12 January 2026" for English. "pattern" is part of "f:format.date" since TYPO3 12.3 (Feature #100187, "ICU-based date and time formatting"), on both core versions this template serves. The date column is a native "DATE" and is required on this relation; an entry without one still renders, without its "time". The section "Marker" is where the axis of an entry is decided. An entry with a usable "icon" - the item's icon the features, the figures and the steps show - shows it in ".theme-timeline__icon", and the stylesheet drops the ring for that item. The icon is rendered with "optional" into a variable first, as in "ThemeSteps.html": a name the set no longer has leaves the ring, not an empty slot on the line.
