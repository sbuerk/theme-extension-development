Backend layout "bands" (Configuration/PageTsConfig/BackendLayouts/Bands.tsconfig) - "main" (colPos 0), "secondary" (colPos 3) and "tertiary" (colPos 4) as three full width bands, one below the other. No breadcrumb and no stage: this is a landing layout, and its first band is what a stage would otherwise hold. ".theme-page__bands" is what takes the page out of its content container. "layout/_page.scss" reacts to it with ":has()" on ".theme-page__body" - the same way the shell reacts to an aside - rather than this template setting a modifier on an element it does not write. The alternative, a band breaking out with "width: 100vw" and a negative margin, was rejected: "100vw" includes the scrollbar, so it overflows by its width on every platform that reserves one, and the acceptance suite measures exactly that on "/layouts/bands" itself ("document.documentElement.scrollWidth" in "Tests/Acceptance/frontend.spec.ts"). Each band is an element of its own so a band can be spaced without reaching into the content elements inside it. The bands themselves are flush, which is what makes them read as bands; the space between two of them is the "space after" an editor sets on the last element of the one above.
